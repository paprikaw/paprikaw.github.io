About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Faculty of Engineering and Information Technology at the University of Melbourne. My research focuses on efficient systems for large-scale AI, particularly multi-GPU LLM serving, runtime resource management, and model parallelism.

I developed PipeLive, a system for live, in-place pipeline-parallel reconfiguration and dynamic KV-cache management, accepted at ACM SoCC 2026. I am currently interested in Mixture-of-Experts systems, including expert parallelism and attention–expert disaggregation, and efficient infrastructure for both inference and training.

Research Interests

  • LLM serving: model parallelism, live reconfiguration, and elastic KV-cache management
  • MoE systems: expert parallelism and attention–expert disaggregation
  • Distributed AI infrastructure: GPU resource management and efficient model execution
